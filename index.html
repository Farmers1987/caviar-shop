About the caviar

Fresh, unpasteurised, from one family farm

Origin

Antonius Caviar raises its sturgeon on its own family farms in Poland. The fish spend the last stage in artesian water, and every tin can be traced back to the individual fish.

How it is made

Prepared the traditional malossol way, with only a little salt. The caviar is fresh and unpasteurised, packed by hand and checked for colour, size, taste and consistency.

How to serve

Open the tin just before serving. Serve it in glass or crystal, or in the tin on crushed ice, and use a mother-of-pearl spoon rather than silver.

Keep it cold

Store unopened tins in the fridge at −3 to +2 °C. Never freeze caviar. Once opened, enjoy within 48 hours. The best-before date is printed on each tin.
