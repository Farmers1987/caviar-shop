6. When the parcel arrives

Please check the parcel when it arrives and put the tins in the fridge straight away, at −3 to +2 °C. Never freeze caviar. Once a tin is opened, enjoy it within 48 hours.

Caviar cannot be returned. Caviar is a perishable food that keeps for a short time and is sealed for hygiene reasons. Under Lithuanian and EU consumer law, the 14-day right of withdrawal therefore does not apply to it. This does not affect your rights if the caviar is faulty (see section 8).

  • We refund the price of the returned item within 14 days of receiving your withdrawal, using the same payment method. We may wait to refund until we have the item back or you have shown that you sent it. If you return everything in your order, we also refund the standard delivery charge.
