Who is responsible

Farmers and Company, UAB, company code 306143862, J. Basanavičiaus g. 26, Vilnius, Lithuania, is responsible for the personal data collected in this shop. Questions about your data: sp@farmersandcompany.lt.

What we collect

  • Your name, email, phone number and delivery address.
  • Your note for the courier, if you write one.
  • What you ordered, what you paid and when.
  • Messages you send us, for example about a complaint or a return.

Card details go straight to Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.

Why we use it

  • To handle your order: taking payment, delivering the parcel, sending the order confirmation and helping you with questions, returns and complaints. This is needed to fulfil our contract with you.
  • To keep accounts: we must keep invoices and payment records under Lithuanian accounting and tax law.
  • To protect our business: for example to prevent payment fraud or to deal with a legal claim. This is our legitimate interest.

Newsletter (only if you sign up): if you sign up for offers by email, we use your email address to send you news about offers and fresh stock. You confirm your sign-up by email first. The legal basis is your consent; you can unsubscribe at any time with the link in every email. We do not sell your data.

Who we share it with

We use these companies to run the shop. They may use your data only for the job they do for us.

  • Stripe: payment.
  • DPD Lietuva: delivery. DPD receives your name, address, phone number, email and courier note.
  • Resend: sending the order emails.
  • MailerLite: our newsletter, only if you sign up.
  • Vercel: hosting of this website.
  • Google (Analytics and Tag Manager): website statistics, only if you accept statistics cookies.

Some of these companies may process data outside the EU/EEA. In that case the transfer is protected by an EU adequacy decision or the EU standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

We keep order and payment records for as long as Lithuanian accounting and tax law requires. Other data, such as emails about an order, we delete when it is no longer needed, at the latest 3 years after your last order.

Cookies

When you first visit the shop, we ask which cookies you accept. You can change your choice at any time under Cookie settings at the bottom of every page.

  • Necessary (always on): your basket, your language and your cookie choice are saved in your own browser so the shop works. Stripe's payment page uses its own cookies, which are needed for a safe payment.
  • Statistics (only with your consent): Google Analytics, loaded through Google Tag Manager, shows us how visitors use the site, for example which products are viewed and added to the basket and whether a purchase is completed. Google sets cookies such as _ga and _ga_…, which are kept for up to 2 years. Google may process the data outside the EU/EEA, protected as described above.
  • Marketing (only with your consent): cookies that help us measure our ads and show relevant offers. We list each tool here before we start using it.

Until you accept, nothing from Google is loaded. If you reject, only necessary cookies are used. The legal basis for statistics and marketing cookies is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

Your rights

You can ask to see the data we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, limit how we use it, object to its use, or get a copy to take elsewhere. Some data we must keep by law even if you ask us to delete it. Write to sp@farmersandcompany.lt; we answer within one month.

If you think we handle your data wrongly, you can complain to the State Data Protection Inspectorate (Valstybinė duomenų apsaugos inspekcija) at vdai.lrv.lt.
